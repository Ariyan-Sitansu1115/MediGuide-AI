    💊 Antibiotic Awareness

    🦠 Viral Infections

    • Caused by viruses (cold, flu, COVID-19)
    • Antibiotics do NOT work on viruses
    • Usually self-limiting with rest & fluids
    • Examples: common cold, influenza, most sore throats

    🧫 Bacterial Infections

    • Caused by bacteria (strep, some pneumonias)
    • May require antibiotics — only when prescribed
    • Always complete the full prescribed course
    • Examples: strep throat, bacterial pneumonia, UTIs
    ⚠️ Antibiotic misuse contributes to antibiotic resistance. Never take antibiotics without a doctor's prescription. Never share or stop antibiotics early.
